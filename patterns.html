Derived components shape these blocks.

A third of all component records in this library are derived rather than stated by a source, and the standard mineral and oxygen base is by construction present in almost every medium. Blocks that look like a universal biochemical core are partly that convention. Which components are derived.

How to read it

A dark cell means the compound is in nearly every medium of that group. A vertical band is a source database's signature: laboratory recipes carry amino acids and vitamins, food databases carry sugars and lipids. The dendrograms order the axes by how alike their profiles are; they are not a claim that the groups are biologically related.

How to read it

Bright square blocks on the diagonal are sets of compounds that are added together: a mineral base, the amino acids, a vitamin panel, a lipid fraction. Some of that structure is real recipe design and some of it is the standard base present in almost every record, which is why the two cannot be separated by eye here.
